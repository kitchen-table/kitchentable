It works.

You are looking at a folder on someone’s computer, served straight to this screen.

This app is not on the internet

It is being served from that computer, over your own network. When the computer sleeps, this page stops answering — nothing about it lives anywhere else.

Make it yours

  1. Open the Welcome folder in your workspace and edit index.html.
  2. Reload this page. That is the whole loop — there is no build step.
  3. Drop any other folder into the workspace and it becomes an app too.
